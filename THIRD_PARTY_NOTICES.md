## V mascot research

The official V mascot artwork in [vlang/v-mascot](https://github.com/vlang/v-mascot)
is licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).
The Veasel Code companion in `tui/assets/veasel-companion-pixel-v8.png` is a
pixel-art reinterpretation of project-owned Veasel concept artwork, generated
independently from a text-only prompt. The V mascot artwork was not used as an
image input. The TUI's 32×32 terminal-palette portrait is reduced from this
companion and adjusts eye pixels for readability at terminal size. Both assets
are distributed under this repository's MIT license. No upstream mascot file
is included or adapted.
